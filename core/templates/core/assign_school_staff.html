About School Staff

School Staff are users who work at specific schools, such as:

  • Teachers
  • Principals
  • School counselors
  • Administrative staff

The school assignment below is created in the same step. Further schools can be added later from the staff member's page.

The staff type is set automatically: Teachers become teaching staff, any other group becomes non-teaching staff.
